Who are we?

Inspired by the digital nomads we’ve followed on social media for so long, we finally decided to take the leap and leave behind our home and jobs in the UK.

For us, 2018 will be a year of working, travelling and learning (and eating). We have no idea where our journey will take us, but we want to share our adventure.

About Joanna

I’m a copywriter, originally from Crewe. When I’m not writing, I can usually be found eating or planning my next holiday. I’ve always loved to travel, but I’m hoping that 2018 will teach me to go with the flow instead of planning Every. Little. Detail.

About Karl

I’m a front-end developer, originally from Warrington. I’ve also played football at a pro and semi-pro level. My first taste of the travelling life was a 2-month charity cycle from San Diego to New York City in 2010. I’m looking forward to a more comfortable journey this time around!
